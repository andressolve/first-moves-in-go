Play a Friend

A full game of Go for two people sitting at the same screen. The board enforces every rule, including captures, suicide and ko, and counts the score when the game ends.

How a game goes

  1. Choose the settings, then press “Start new game”. Beginners should start on 9×9. A 9×9 game takes about 15 minutes, 13×13 about 40, and 19×19 an hour or more.
  2. Decide who plays which colour. Black moves first. If one of you is stronger, the weaker player takes Black.
  3. Take turns clicking the board. The last move is marked with a small circle. If you misclick, press “Undo” to take back the last move.
  4. Pass when there is nothing useful left to play. When both players pass one after the other, the game ends.
  5. Agree on dead stones. The board guesses which stones are dead and shows them faded. If you disagree, click a group to switch it between dead and alive. Then press “Accept the result”. If you can’t agree, press “Keep playing” and settle it on the board.

The board saves an unfinished game in this browser, so you can close the page and carry on later.

Komi and handicap stones

Komi is the number of points White receives for moving second. The standard is 7.5 with the area scoring used in these lessons. The half point means a game can never end in a tie.

Handicap stones make a game fair between players of different strength. Black places 2 to 9 stones on the star points before the game starts, and then White moves first. With a handicap, komi is usually 0.5, and the board switches to that for you when you choose a handicap. A rough guide: give one extra stone for each game in a row the stronger player wins, and take one away for each game the weaker player wins.
